Reviewed against the source corpus on 2026-07-18.

This document is a compact navigation layer for retrieval. It summarizes the

For exact sequences, dialogue, visual details, powers, or canon-sensitive

## Status vocabulary

- **Established:** Shown in a numbered published chapter or stated as canon in
  a dedicated reference document.
- **Reference:** Defined by a character or lore sheet but not necessarily shown
  on-page yet.
- **Adjacent story:** Present in an unnumbered story; its exact placement in the
  main chronology may be unspecified.
- **Planned:** Described only in a framework or future-facing reference.
- **Inference:** A useful reading that is not directly established.
- **Conflict:** Current sources do not cleanly agree; do not resolve it without
  a creator ruling.

All source files currently lack explicit `context_status` metadata except for
status inferred from filenames. These labels are therefore editorial navigation,
not machine-certified canon.

## Universe at a glance

The Superbear Universe centers on **Bearing**, a transformative force through
which desire, belief, and choice reshape a person into a larger, stronger,
hairier, more fully embodied form. Normal Bearing creates an extraordinary but
non-superpowered bear body. Super Bearing continues beyond embodiment when the
chosen life requires powers, public visibility, danger, and responsibility.
The central ethical divide is not whether transformation occurs, but whether
power is invited and integrated or imposed through ownership. (`lore/bearing.md`)

Mike accidentally receives the entire remaining reserve of Daddy Bear's power
through the only active cup of Power Bear Coffee and becomes Superbear. Nick
later makes an informed choice to share Mike's dangerous public life and becomes
Supercub. Their partnership embodies strength without hierarchy and love without
surrender. (`stories/the-origin-of-superbear.md`,
`stories/the-origin-of-supercub.md`,
`lore/relationship_superbear_supercub.md`)

The primary antagonist is Bill, formerly the chosen cub of the ancient guardian
Ursus Rex. Using the Seal, Bill forcibly stole Rex's active power and became
Butch Bear. He built the Brotherhood around dominion, hierarchy, addictive
reward, and corrupted Bearing. (`lore/fall_of_daddy_bear.md`,
`lore/the_seal_lore.md`, `characters/butchbear_character_sheet_revised_2.md`)

At the current end of the published main series, Nick has been corrupted into
Bill's powerful partner and conversion engine. Mike has tracked Nick's movements,
Andrew/Super Orso has returned from Italy with a sample of transported corrupted
material, and both heroes have identified a Black Ursa warehouse concert as the
next operational target. (`stories/ch14.md`, `stories/ch15.md`,
`stories/ch16.md`)

## Core rules and concepts

### Bearing

- Bearing answers desire and willingness, not moral worthiness.
- The body grows in mass, muscle, hair, confidence, and presence.
- A completed Bearing is permanent; it is not a temporary costume change.
- Bearing is not ordinary healing, though a fully reshaped body may no longer
  share the prior body's limitations.
- Normal Bearing ends at embodied human capability. Super Bearing continues
  into superhuman strength, flight or other abilities when required by the life
  consciously chosen.
- Superbear and Supercub can offer Normal Bearing to others without exhausting
  their own power, but they cannot simply manufacture another Super Bearer.
- Consent-based Bearing expands identity and autonomy. Corrupted Bearing narrows
  choice and reorganizes the person around external dominion.

Primary source: `lore/bearing.md`.

### Power Bear Coffee and rainbow power

- Daddy Bear condensed his final remaining power into rainbow dust and hid it in
  one box of ordinary coffee.
- Brewing released the entire reserve into the first cup; the remaining grounds
  became inert.
- Mike consumed that unregulated reserve unknowingly, producing accidental
  Super Bearing and the capacity to bear others.
- Rainbow energy is associated with Mike and Nick's consent-based modern form of
  Bearing; Rex's older power does not use the same visual signature.

Primary sources: `lore/bearing.md`, `lore/fall_of_daddy_bear.md`,
`characters/rex_character_sheet.md`.

### Consent-based Bearing

- Power is invited through explicit choice and internal alignment.
- The resulting body and suit express the transformed person's identity rather
  than an owner's design.
- Love can clarify what someone wants, but never substitutes for consent.
- Superbear and Supercub are caretakers of transformation, not authorities who
  decide who another person should become.

Primary sources: `lore/bearing.md`,
`lore/relationship_superbear_supercub.md`.

### Corrupted Bearing and the Brotherhood

Butch Bear's dominion compresses perceived options and makes hierarchy feel like
relief. Consent may be verbal yet incomplete because consequences are hidden or
the target is already under influence. Force produces less stable corruption
than willing acceptance. (`lore/bearing.md`)

The documented hierarchy is:

- **Alpha:** Butch Bear/Bill, source and absolute authority.
- **Sirs:** Intelligent commanders transformed primarily through cigar smoke.
  Their judgment and ego are preserved but redirected; they are rewarded both
  for commanding Boys and serving Bill.
- **Boys:** Interchangeable enforcers whose autonomy is suppressed. They require
  harnesses to stabilize obedience.

Harnesses are conduits and enforcement systems, not the original power source.
They reward compliance through addictive pleasure and make autonomy feel like
withdrawal. A freely accepted harness tends to integrate more stably than one
applied by force. (`lore/bearing.md`,
`characters/butchbear_character_sheet_revised_2.md`,
`characters/evan_character_sheet.md`)

### The Seal

- The Seal is an ancient marked stone predating Rex and perhaps the current
  guardianship system.
- It amplifies what its holder brings to a transformation threshold. In Rex's
  hands it authenticated consent; in Bill's hands it enabled forced transfer and
  amplified his existing flaws into doctrine.
- Bill reads its mirrored markings as “BB,” built his identity around them, and
  wears the Seal inside the badge of his harness.
- It stabilizes power that Bill's body was never meant to hold and sustains his
  corrupted system, including Nick's suit.
- The lore says destroying it would destabilize Brotherhood corruptions and free
  a properly grounded Nick, but the physical means of destruction is undefined.

Primary sources: `lore/the_seal_lore.md`,
`lore/fall_of_daddy_bear.md`.

### Mike and Nick's relationship rules

- They are romantic partners first and superheroes second.
- Their public “hero and sidekick” framing reflects experience, not authority or
  worth; Supercub is a hero in training and the relationship has no fixed
  hierarchy.
- Mike is expressive, humorous, decisive, and prone to protection-through-
  control. Nick is reflective, analytical, ethically focused, and prone to
  hesitation or going alone after feeling overruled.
- Protection without truth or trust is a violation. Neither strength nor fear
  grants unilateral decision-making rights.
- Their recurring red lines are forced compliance, withholding truth to control
  outcomes, transformation without explicit consent, and ownership symbolism.
- Repair happens through honest conversation, quiet physical closeness, and
  humor that releases pressure without dismissing harm.

Primary source: `lore/relationship_superbear_supercub.md`.

## Character directory

### Mike / Superbear

Mike begins as an emotionally muted barista who is kind, curious, and open but
largely drifting through life. Accidental Super Bearing makes him massive,
joyful, visible, extremely strong, durable, able to fly, and capable of Bearing
others. His warmth and playful bear puns make his power reassuring rather than
threatening. His central flaw is confusing protection with the right to decide
for someone else, particularly Nick. (`characters/mike_character_sheet_pre_transformation.md`,
`characters/superbear_character_sheet_post_transformation.md`)

### Nick / Supercub

Nick begins as a gentle insurance claims worker whose stable life conflicts with
his ethics and whose body and future feel paused. He chooses Mike and the public,
dangerous life around him with informed consent, becoming the immensely strong,
flying, grounding Supercub. He specializes in investigation, pattern recognition,
rescue, and support. His empathy and moral clarity remain central, but feeling
overprotected contributes to his decision to investigate the Brotherhood alone.
(`characters/nick_character_sheet_revised.md`,
`characters/supercub_character_sheet.md`)

### Corrupted Supercub

Bill captures Nick, suppresses his powers with a wristband, and covers his
existing suit and body with corrupted material produced from Bill himself. The
result is a glossy black-and-grey suit, violet eyes, amplified dark power, and a
state organized around devotion to Bill. Bill uses him both as an intimate trophy
and as an army engine whose bearer capacity can convert people faster and at
larger scale. His underlying consent-based energy introduces structural
instability into what he creates, even when his conscious corrupted state feels
complete. (`characters/corrupted_supercub_character_sheet.md`,
`stories/the-corruption-of-supercub.md`)

### Bill / Butch Bear

Bill was Ursus Rex's chosen cub. Rex failed him in an unspecified but damaging
way; Bill's resentment, impatience, and hunger for completion were later amplified
by the Seal. He stole Rex's power and became Butch Bear, whose ideology treats
choice as inefficient and surrender as mercy. His presence exerts passive
dominion, and his methods include seduction, coercion, force, harnesses, cigar
smoke, wristbands, and bodily corrupted material. His blind spot is that he
cannot understand freely chosen connection as a form of power.
(`characters/butchbear_character_sheet_revised_2.md`,
`lore/fall_of_daddy_bear.md`)

### Rex / Ursus Rex / Daddy Bear

Rex is the ancient former guardian of transformation: a living threshold who
could move people between forms but built his identity around consent. Bill's
theft left him diminished and unable to restore himself or ask for power. He
placed his last active power in Power Bear Coffee, then withdrew to a cabin
northwest of the city. His possible future is renewal freely offered by others,
not restoration of his former authority. (`characters/rex_character_sheet.md`,
`lore/fall_of_daddy_bear.md`)

### Andrew / Super Orso

Andrew is a twenty-year-old Italian exchange student: intelligent, observant,
socially tentative, physically insecure, and vulnerable to promised certainty.
The Brotherhood targets and corrupts him as a useful asset. Superbear breaks the
control during a fight, after which Mike and Nick help him recover through slow,
consent-based energy. Andrew later intervenes when he recognizes the same
recruitment pattern being used on another young man; the accumulated healing
energy answers his protective choice and transforms him into Super Orso.
Super Orso is an Italy-based hero with great strength, explosive ground mobility,
and blue energy constructs shaped by an Italian/Roman martial vocabulary.
(`characters/andrew_character_sheet.md`,
`characters/super_orso_character_sheet.md`,
`stories/remaking-andrew.md`, `stories/andrew-il-super-orso.md`)

### Evan

Evan is Nick's trusted friend and a calm, socially grounded metal fan. He is
forcibly converted during the Chapter 5 concert night and reappears in Chapter 7
as a harnessed Brotherhood Boy. His memories of Nick are suppressed rather than
erased, making him a potential fracture point. Rescue requires addressing the
harness's addiction loop, not merely removing an object. (`characters/evan_character_sheet.md`)

### Adam

Adam is a disciplined, socially fluent gym-goer exhausted by continuous
self-monitoring and self-authorship. In Chapter 4, Bill offers relief through a
harness. Adam accepts without understanding the metaphysical cost, undergoes a
stable corrupted Bearing, and leaves as an obedient Boy. His explicit but
incomplete consent demonstrates the ambiguity and danger of Bill's preferred
method. (`characters/adam_character_sheet.md`,
`stories/the-last-decision.md`)

### Anthony / Antonio

Anthony is gentle, observant, and resigned to remaining adjacent to the bear
embodiment he desires. After Bearing, Antonio remains the same kind person but
becomes large, hairy, confident, flirtatious, and joyfully at home in bear
culture. He represents fulfillment without heroism or destiny. A later story
suggests his bodily fluids can accidentally transmit transformation, a power not
defined in his character sheet. (`characters/anthony_character_sheet.md`,
`characters/antonio_character_sheet.md`,
`stories/the-night-anthony-became-antonio.md`, `stories/antonio1.md`)

### Story-only characters

- **Chris:** The first person Superbear deliberately Bears. He gives immediate
  consent after being rescued and receives Normal Bearing, not superpowers.
  (`stories/bear-you-out.md`)
- **Arun:** A lonely bar patron converted by corrupted Nick into a black-rubber
  Brotherhood recruit. (`stories/the-conversion.md`)
- **Dan:** An Australian visitor recruited through Prowlr and converted by
  corrupted Nick in a park while Mike observes covertly. (`stories/ch14.md`)
- **Jacob:** Nick's long-distance friend. A sincere hug accidentally triggers
  Normal Bearing; Jacob is happy with the outcome, while Nick recognizes the
  ethical weight of causing a permanent change unintentionally.
  (`stories/how-jacob-became-a-bear.md`)
- **Thom:** Antonio's date, transformed during an explicit sexual encounter.
  The event reveals Antonio may transmit Bearing without knowing how to control
  it. (`stories/antonio1.md`)
- **Colin and Nico:** Roommates in an apparently separate Prowlr+ premise. Nico
  uses a cracked app's body editor on Colin; the transformation becomes permanent
  and grants Colin a potentially transmissible influence over Nico.
  (`stories/prowlr-part-one.md`)

## Main-series chronology

Publication dates are metadata; chapter numbers define the narrative sequence.

1. **The origin of Superbear:** Mike drinks the only active cup of Power Bear
   Coffee and undergoes accidental Super Bearing. (`stories/the-origin-of-superbear.md`)
2. **Bear you out:** Superbear rescues Chris, explains permanence, receives his
   consent, and performs his first Normal Bearing. (`stories/bear-you-out.md`)
3. **Shared Caffeine and Property Damage:** Nick meets Mike at a coffee shop,
   asks him on a date, and remains unaware that Mike leaves to answer a fire as
   Superbear. (`stories/shared-caffeine-and-property-damage.md`)
4. **The Last Decision:** Bill recruits Adam at a late-night gym through
   psychological insight and an offered harness. (`stories/the-last-decision.md`)
5. **The corruption:** Mike's protective distance hurts Nick. Nick joins Evan at
   a Black Ursa warehouse concert, witnesses a Brotherhood conversion, and is
   knocked unconscious after entering a restricted area.
   (`stories/the-corruption.md`)
6. **Protective Bear Instinct:** Mike senses Nick's fear and sudden emotional
   silence, recognizes that distance has failed, transforms, and tracks him by
   scent. (`stories/protective-bear-instinct.md`)
7. **The Wrong Room:** Nick wakes restrained, discovers Evan has become a
   Brotherhood enforcer, meets Bill, and is rescued by Superbear. Bill identifies
   Nick as Mike's vulnerability. (`stories/the-wrong-room.md`)
8. **Choosing You:** Mike's grief exposes his identity. Nick recognizes him,
   chooses to remain with him, and establishes “Bear” and “Cub” as mutual terms
   of affection. (`stories/choosing-you.md`)
9. **The Origin of Supercub:** After recognizing that permanent protection would
   itself be a cage, Nick makes an informed choice to share Mike's life and
   danger. Mike Bears him and the transformation continues into Super Bearing.
   (`stories/the-origin-of-supercub.md`)
10. **Training Day:** Nick explores his new body, durability, suit, flight, and
    strength control. Sensing Evan turns training into a commitment to investigate
    the Brotherhood and rescue others. (`stories/training-day.md`)
11. **The corruption of Supercub:** Feeling repeatedly overruled, Nick infiltrates
    a Brotherhood facility alone to save Evan. He is captured, power-suppressed,
    and transformed by Bill into corrupted Supercub, sending one final pulse to
    Mike before the corruption closes. (`stories/the-corruption-of-supercub.md`)
12. **The Old Bear:** Five weeks later, Mike finds Daddy Bear/Rex through the
    scent embedded in the original note. Rex explains that Bill buries rather
    than erases a person and teaches Mike to find Nick through stillness and
    connection. (`stories/the-old-bear.md`)
13. **The Conversion:** Set weeks earlier, corrupted Nick converts Arun in an
    alley. The act rewards Nick and establishes his repeatable conversion role.
    (`stories/the-conversion.md`)
14. **A Good Boy:** Corrupted Nick uses Prowlr to lure and convert Dan. Mike
    watches from a distance, sees no conscious sign of the Nick he knows, and
    asks Andrew for help. (`stories/ch14.md`)
15. **The Regular:** In Naples, Super Orso discovers a Brotherhood logistics hub,
    international shipments, and bottles of corrupted material. He receives
    Mike's message about Nick. (`stories/ch15.md`)
16. **Meeting an old friend:** Andrew returns with a sample. He and Mike combine
    the international shipment evidence, Mike's surveillance, and a Black Ursa
    poster to identify a Saturday warehouse concert as their next move.
    (`stories/ch16.md`)

## Planned Chapter 17 — do not treat as completed

`stories/ch17_framework.md` is a framework, not a published numbered chapter.
It plans to retell the approach to the Black Ursa concert from corrupted Nick's
close-third-person perspective. Nick is intended to manage Brotherhood logistics
as Bill's general, feel genuine pride and devotion, and participate with Bill in
a mass conversion called the Passionate Union. The planned ending is Mike and
Andrew breaking through the roof before anyone reacts. Outcomes, dialogue, the
heroes' exposure to the wave, and the response to their intrusion remain
undecided.

The framework explicitly requires:

- no conscious captive Nick narrating from behind the corruption;
- accurate memory with altered emotional meaning rather than amnesia;
- a correction/reward sequence between Bill and Nick;
- no Evan appearance;
- no Seal resolution;
- a hard cut on Mike and Andrew's arrival.

## Continuity, conflict, and source-gap register

### Nick's awareness during corruption — conflict

The corrupted Supercub sheet says there is no conscious Nick watching from
behind the corruption and the Chapter 17 framework locks that interpretation.
The same character sheet's aftermath section also says Nick was conscious and
aware throughout, unable to act. These are materially different models of his
experience. Use the no-conscious-observer model for planned Chapter 17 because it
is explicitly locked there, but do not generalize it backward or forward without
a creator ruling. (`characters/corrupted_supercub_character_sheet.md`,
`stories/ch17_framework.md`)

### SERVE-triggered surfacing — clarified but not fully integrated

The crossover gives Nick a brief full return to self away from Mike. The Chapter
17 framework calls it an involuntary, externally triggered surfacing that differs
from the later intended relational surfacing with Mike. It also says this should
eventually be added to a rescue addendum, but that addendum is absent.
(`stories/expansion.md`, `stories/ch17_framework.md`)

### What Rex told Mike about the Seal — chronology gap

The Seal lore says Rex gives Mike the complete explanation when Mike finds him.
Published Chapter 12 shows Rex explaining corruption and teaching Mike to find
Nick, but it does not show the Seal explanation. The Chapter 17 framework also
reserves Seal material for later. Therefore do not assume Mike already knows the
Seal's location or destruction mechanics after Chapter 16 unless the creator
rules that the missing conversation occurred off-page. (`lore/the_seal_lore.md`,
`stories/the-old-bear.md`, `stories/ch17_framework.md`)

### Missing referenced sources

The Chapter 17 framework cites files not present in the current corpus:

- `brotherhood_army_lore.md`
- `corrupted_supercub_rescue_addendum.md`

It also uses older project-local path names such as `ch11.md`. Use the current

claims dependent only on absent files as unavailable rather than reconstructing

### Unspecified continuities

The Prowlr+ story and some transformation-focused stories do not declare whether
they share the main chronology. Do not merge their mechanics into the main
Bearing system solely because similar transformations occur. In particular,
Antonio's “bear factory” transmission and Prowlr+'s app mechanics should remain
story-specific until a creator ruling connects them.
