# Colin — Character Sheet

*(Prowlr+ spinoff / Antonio-AU continuity — appearance & identity reference)*

---

## Basic Information
- **Name:** Colin
- **Age:** 27 (reads younger — sweet, sleepy, boyish)
- **Role:** Nico's roommate; the transformation subject of Part One
- **Life status:** Student who works shifts (introduced coming off a double shift)
- **Orientation:** Deliberately unfixed pre-transformation. The app *sets* his sexuality slider to "men" — he does not start the story established as gay. Treat his before-state as unlabeled/ambiguous; the change makes it definite.

---

## The Core Idea
Colin is the soft puppy who becomes the mountain. He should read as gentle and easy to underestimate before, so the endpoint — the huge, heavily furred daddy — lands as a genuine inversion. His transformation is **massing + furring**, not a change of person or coloring: his hair color and his eyes both stay the same the whole way through. What changes is *density and mass* — the beard fills in, the pelt grows, the frame swells — never the color. That constancy is what keeps him recognizably Colin.

## Appearance — BEFORE (baseline)

**Build:** Slim-soft, "dad-bod-in-training." Untoned, a little softness at the waist, rounded shoulders, no muscle definition. Not skinny, not big — an unremarkable everyman softness.

**Face & features:** Soft, boyish lines. Gentle jaw, a bit of youthful roundness in the cheeks. Open, sleepy expression.

**Eyes:** Warm hazel — green-brown. *(Identity anchor: eyes do not change through the transformation.)*

**Hair:** Warm brown, floppy soft waves, a little overgrown. *(Same shade throughout — see After / note.)*

**Facial hair:** Patchy stubble he can't yet fill in — sparse, uneven. (Sets up the beard beat: this is a face that *shouldn't* be able to grow a beard.)

**Body hair:** Minimal, fair, near-smooth chest and arms.

**Skin:** Fair-to-medium, warm undertone, smooth.

**Clothing:** A washed-out sage-green henley, soft and shapeless. Comfortable, unremarkable, doesn't celebrate his body.

**Overall impression:** Sweet, tired, cute-but-forgettable. The kind of guy nobody clocks as a "daddy" in waiting.

## Appearance — AFTER (locked endpoint, from Part One)

**Build:** Huge and broad. Broad shoulders, thick slabs of muscle under a soft-solid rounded gut (firm, not sloppy), thick thighs. A "daddy" frame.

**Beard & head hair:** The same warm brown as before — unchanged in color. The sparse stubble fills into a full, wiry beard; the head hair is the same shade it always was. At full density, against fair skin and in low light, the growth *reads* dark (this is how the story's "dark bristles" is realized) — but it is not a color change.

**Body hair:** Dense pelt in the same warm brown, across chest and stomach, forearms, and the backs of the hands — reads dark at this density. Nipples visible in the pelt.

**Eyes:** Still warm hazel — unchanged. The single through-line.

**Voice:** Deep, gravelly baritone.

**Register:** Commanding, possessive, "daddy energy" — protective and controlling at once.

## Contrast note (with Nico)
Before the change, Colin is the *softer, younger-reading* of the two and Nico is the sharper one. The transformation flips that hierarchy. Their coloring separates them cleanly and diverges further as Colin changes: Colin is warm brown + fair skin, deepening into a dense pelt; Nico is bleached blonde + olive skin, staying light. Hair and skin both carry the contrast.
