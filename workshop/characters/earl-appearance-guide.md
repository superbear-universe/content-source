# Earl — Appearance Guide

## Visual overview
Earl is a powerfully built man in his late forties, fair-skinned but ruddy from years of sun and cold, built like a strongman rather than a sculpted athlete — raw, dense muscle over a solid frame, with just enough belly to ground him rather than soften him. His face is heavy and still: a square jaw, a permanent heavy brow, and steady, unreadable eyes that rarely change. A thick, untrimmed dark beard, greying at the temples like the hair above it, frames a straight nose that was once broken and never quite reset. He plants himself wide and doesn't shift his weight. Nothing about him performs size — he simply occupies it.

## Scale and silhouette
- Apparent age: Late 40s — weathered, not old
- Height: Similar to his sons (already large men); wins on density and mass rather than added height
- Build: Powerlifter/strongman — raw functional bulk, less defined than a sculpted physique, more brute mass
- Proportions: Broad shoulders tapering hard to a relatively narrow waist (a blunted V, despite the belly); torso slightly long and deep; limbs proportionally shorter and thick; hands large but proportional, not a focal anchor
- Posture and stance: Wide, grounded stance; weight carried low; rarely shifts position; stillness reads as authority

## Head and face
- Head and face shape: Square, heavy-jawed, blunt
- Brow and eyebrows: Heavy, permanent brow — reads stern even at complete rest
- Eyes: Steady and direct; plain, watchful, no theatrical intensity
- Nose: Straight, once broken, never fully reset
- Mouth: Full lips, typically held in a flat, unreadable line
- Ears: Unremarkable, proportional, no distinguishing detail
- Distinctive asymmetry or marks: One or two visible scars (forearm, hand, or brow) as quiet evidence of a working life

## Hair and facial hair
- Hairline and parting: Receding slightly at the temples, no defined part
- Length and silhouette: Short, practical, slightly overgrown — cut for work, not style
- Texture and styling: Left unstyled; functional rather than groomed
- Colour and placement: Dark brown, greying at the temples
- Facial hair: Full, thick beard, dense and untrimmed but not wild or unkempt; same dark-brown-greying-at-temples colouring as the head hair

## Skin and anatomy
- Skin tone and undertone: Fair, ruddy from prolonged sun and cold exposure
- Texture and age detail: General weathering consistent with outdoor labor; no itemized callus or knuckle detail beyond that
- Freckles, scars, tattoos, or markings: One or two visible scars (forearm, hand, or brow)
- Body hair: Dense on chest and arms; lighter elsewhere on the body
- Nonhuman or synthetic anatomy: None — Earl is fully human in appearance; his symbiotic nature is internal/biological and has no visible non-human markers

## Current outfit
- Garments and layers: Flannel or henley shirt, sleeves rolled or pushed up; suspenders (default) or a work belt depending on scene; canvas or denim work pants
- Fit and construction: Loose enough to move in, snug across the chest, shoulders, and belly where his mass fills the fabric
- Materials and surface finish: Heavy cotton flannel/knit, canvas or denim, worn leather suspenders
- Wear and condition: Working clothes — creased, sun-faded, honestly worn, never pristine
- Footwear: Heavy logging boots
- Accessories: None — no jewelry, purely functional dress

## Colour and material palette
- Primary: Deep forest green, brown, black
- Secondary: Faded denim blue (canvas/denim pants)
- Accent: None — deliberately unadorned
- Material response: Matte, worn textiles; no shine or polish; leather suspenders show age and flex creases

## Recognition anchors
1. Powerlifter-strongman mass carried with a slight, grounding belly (not soft, not dominant)
2. Heavy, permanent brow paired with a steady, unreadable gaze and a flat-line mouth
3. Full, thick, untrimmed dark beard, greying at the temples to match the head hair
4. Straight nose, once broken
5. Wide, planted stance — he does not shift his weight
6. Dense chest and arm hair, lighter elsewhere — the visual "endpoint" his sons gradually drift toward as they integrate

## Canon flexibility

### Locked

- Jaw, eyes, brow, nose, mouth
- Hair colour, length/style, hairline, beard construction, body hair density pattern
- Skin tone, scarring, general weathering
- Core wardrobe garments, palette, no-accessories rule

### Flexible
- Choice of suspenders vs. work belt per scene (suspenders proposed as default)
- Whether he wears the flannel buttoned, open, or as a henley in a given scene
- Degree of visible weathering/texture close-up detail if a scene calls for it

### Undecided
- None of consequence

## Consistency constraints
- Earl's height must always read as comparable to his sons' — the visual distinction is mass/density, never a head-and-shoulders height advantage.
- The belly must remain "slight/grounding," never soft-dominant and never absent — this is a specific, locked middle point.
- Beard and head hair must always share the same dark-brown-greying-at-temples colouring; do not render one darker or greyer than the other independently.
- Hairline recession is at the temples only — do not extend to a bald crown or full baldness.
- Body hair density is chest/arms = dense, elsewhere = lighter — do not render him uniformly hairy or hairless.
- The nose's break/asymmetry should be subtle — a straight nose with quiet history, not a dramatically crooked one.
- Scars (forearm/hand/brow) should appear in the same location across images once a specific placement is chosen for a reference sheet.
- No jewelry, no tattoos, no decorative accessories in any depiction.
- Wide, weight-low stance should be preserved in any full-body pose; avoid dynamic or off-balance poses that contradict his stillness.
