# Nico — Character Sheet

*(Prowlr+ spinoff / Antonio-AU continuity — appearance & identity reference)*

---

## Basic Information
- **Name:** Nico
- **Age:** 26
- **Role:** Colin's roommate; the instigator who finds and runs the cracked Prowlr+ build. The implied *next* subject.
- **Orientation:** Gay / into men — his arousal during the scene is established, and his pull toward Colin drives the back half of Part One.

---

## The Core Idea
Nico is the wiry, quick, bratty one who pokes at things he shouldn't — and gets more than he bargained for. Before the change he reads as the sharper, more "adult" of the pair; the story's engine is that this hierarchy inverts. He is the tinkerer: phone always in hand, the type to run a bootleg app "to see what it does."

---

## Appearance (baseline)

**Build:** Compact and wiry — smaller in frame than Colin, but more coiled and kinetic. Lean, lightly toned, restless. He takes up less space physically but more attention.

**Face & features:** Angular. Sharp jaw, quick dark eyes, a crooked, mischievous grin. Expressive, a little sly.

**Eyes:** Dark brown, fast-moving.

**Hair:** Bleached blonde — a deliberate dye job, gone a little brassy and grown out, with dark roots showing at the part. Skater/indie length, unkempt, better slept-on than styled. His natural base is dark (the roots and stubble give it away), which is a quiet character tell: he messes with things, including himself.

**Facial hair:** Light stubble he *can* actually grow — a deliberate contrast with Colin, who can't. Before the change, Nico is the "manlier-looking" one.

**Skin:** Olive / Mediterranean, warm-toned (fits the name, and contrasts Colin's fair palette).

**Style — wiry skater / indie scruff:** Loose, worn tees and hoodies, a bit rumpled. Beat-up sneakers. Occasional beanie. Not fitted or polished — but the bleach job says he *does* care how he looks, in a scruffy, offhand way. Hands always busy: phone, fidgets.

**Overall impression:** Clever, kinetic, a touch smug. The instigator you don't quite trust with a new toy.

## Contrast note (with Colin)
Bleached-blonde-and-olive against Colin's warm-brown-and-fair — the two now separate cleanly on both hair and skin, and they diverge *further* as Colin darkens into the pelt while Nico stays light. Sharp against soft. Smaller-but-coiled against larger-but-yielding. Before the change, a stranger would read Nico as the dominant one — which is exactly the expectation Part One overturns.

---

## Transformation trajectory — OPEN
Part One flags Nico as the next subject ("Compatible subject detected: Nico") but does not transform him. His endpoint is undesigned. Two directions to decide before Part Two:

1. **Echo** — same darkening + massing + furring mechanic as Colin, so the app converts a "type." Risk: the two men end up too similar.
2. **Differ** — a distinct endpoint that keeps his wiry, kinetic quality (e.g. lean-and-hard rather than heavy-and-furred, or a different register of transformation entirely), preserving the odd-couple silhouette even after both change.

Recommendation deferred until you decide what Part Two is *about* — whether the horror is "everyone becomes the same thing" (Echo) or "it takes what you are and warps it" (Differ).

*Visual beat available either way:* his dark roots mean a transformation could grow **his** hair/beard/pelt in dark, his real base color reasserting itself as the bleach gets outgrown or overwritten — the app stripping away the affectation he chose and replacing it with what he actually is. A cheap, effective on-screen tell that the change is taking hold.
