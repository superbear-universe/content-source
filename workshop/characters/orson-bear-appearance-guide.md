# Curly-Haired Bearded Man — Appearance Guide

## Visual overview

This fictional human man appears 22–24 years old and stands about 178–182 cm tall. He has a compact, distinctly beary, powerfully built body: dense heavy musculature softened by a natural layer of body fat, a thick neck with full traps, a deep broad ribcage, heavy limbs, moderately broad shoulders, a thick waist, and a softly solid abdomen with little visible V-taper. His identity is anchored by a compact, moderately broad face; muted hazel-green eyes beneath dense brows; an abundant mass of warm medium-brown curls; and a full warm-brown beard with a softly rounded, slightly longer chin section. At rest, his face looks warm, confident, kind, and subtly cheeky rather than blank or stern. His light, warm-toned skin remains mostly clear, while dense natural body hair reinforces the heavy, masculine silhouette. His canonical presentation is barefoot in fitted dark-charcoal boxer briefs.

The neck and traps are full; the chest and ribcage are deep and broad; the waist is thick; the abdomen is softly solid rather than flat, carved, or prominently bellied; and the upper arms, forearms, thighs, and calves all carry substantial weight.

- **Mouth:** Medium-width mouth with a softly defined Cupid's bow, a moderate upper lip, and a slightly fuller lower lip. Natural muted rose-pink lip colour. The beard and moustache must not conceal the basic lip shape.

- **Distinctive asymmetry or marks:** Preserve subtle, ordinary asymmetry in brow height and eyelid opening from the source portrait. Do not add scars, freckles, moles, tattoos, or other signature marks.

## Hair and facial hair

- **Hairline and parting:** Full low-to-medium hairline with softly rounded temples. A loose off-centre part sits on his left; the curls sweep across and forward toward his right, concentrating the largest curl mass above the right side of his forehead.
- **Length and silhouette:** Approximately 8–10 cm through the upper curl mass. The sides and back are full scissor-cut curls around 3–5 cm long, gradually and subtly shortened at the ears and nape. There is no exposed-skin fade, undercut, or sharply disconnected top.
- **Texture and styling:** Very dense, springy, large-to-medium curls with abundant three-dimensional volume and irregular individual ringlets. Styling is controlled enough to hold a flattering silhouette but remains touchable, natural, and slightly tousled.
- **Colour and placement:** Warm medium chestnut-brown overall, with darker roots and lowlights plus subtle golden-brown and caramel variation where curls catch the light. No greying.
- **Facial hair:** Dense connected full beard in warm medium brown, with natural darker and lighter strands. It measures roughly 2–3 cm through the cheeks and jaw and about 3–4 cm at the chin, forming a softly rounded lower silhouette. The moustache is full and connected to the beard but remains proportional and does not overwhelm the mouth. Use a soft natural cheek line and a gently tidied neckline; avoid razor-sharp outlines.
