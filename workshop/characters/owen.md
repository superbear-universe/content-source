# Owen — Character Sheet

## Basic Information
- **Name:** Owen
- **Age:** Mid-to-late 40s
- **Background:** Bullied out of rugby as a young man; drifted into a burnt-out office job and grew heavyset. Transformed into a bear by Mike years later.
- **Bear Transformation:** A transformed bear — Mike changed him at the Paw & Anchor. Whether Owen can transform others himself is deliberately left open (see Origin and Gym / Method).
- **Narrative Role:** Gym owner in Mike's orbit — a downstream example of Mike's transformative influence. Relationship to the wider cast still to be developed.

---

## Origin

As a young man, Owen wanted to play rugby. He tried out and was rejected — laughed off the team and bullied out for not fitting the accepted body type, judged inadequate before he ever got to play. He took the eyebrow scar during that period. The dream died there.

He gave up and settled: a burnt-out office job and a life that felt muted. Over the years he grew heavy, but it was size without strength, confidence, or identity — the mass, and none of what he'd once wanted from it.

Years later he crossed paths with Mike and ended up at the Paw & Anchor. He opened up; Mike listened, and helped him through the transformation. What came out the other side was the prop-forward body he'd been told he could never have — mass and muscle both, finally matching the person inside. The change gave him more than a physique: presence, confidence, warmth, authority, and for the first time, comfort in his own size.

### Pre-Transformation Form
Heavyset and tired — a big man carrying weight without power. Reference art: middle-aged, olive-skinned, dark hair, light-blue button-down shirt and dark jeans, black sneakers. Reads as burnt-out and unhappy in his body — the "size without strength" the transformation later converts into power.

## Appearance

**Build Archetype:** Prop-forward build — a genuine muscle bear frame, not simply heavyset. Barrel-chested and thick-necked, the body of a front-row forward. Owen never actually played; this is the body the transformation gave him — the physique the sport once told him he'd never have.

- **Height:** 6'2"–6'4"
- **Build:** Massive through chest and shoulders — wide everywhere at once rather than tapered. Neck nearly as thick as his jaw. A natural layer of softness sits over it, but the underlying muscle stays dominant: massive slab-like pecs with visible separation, thick heavy traps merging into a bull neck, dense powerful arms with real muscular definition under a layer of natural softness, a wide muscular back. His stomach is a hard, dense "muscle gut" — a solid, rounded mass sitting high and firm, with the faint shadowed suggestion of a four-pack near the ribs. No soft overhang past the waistband, no droop — a strongman/powerlifter gut, not a sedentary one.
- **Posture:** Grounded, low center of gravity even standing still. Doesn't perform his size. Carries himself with an ease he never had before the change — comfortable taking up room for the first time in his life.

### Face
- **Face Shape:** Broad and square-jawed, structure still holding under the beard and weight.
- **Nose:** Straight, unbroken, blunt-tipped.
- **Eyebrows:** Thick, dark, greying at the outer edges. A thin scar splits the **left** eyebrow roughly a third of the way in — his only facial mark, otherwise clean complexion.
- **Eyes:** Hazel (#7a6a4f), deep-set under a heavy brow, faint crow's-feet at the corners, heavier lids giving an unhurried rather than tired look.
- **Mouth:** Full but unfussy, mostly hidden by mustache. Slight natural downturn at rest reads as gruff rather than unhappy; softens considerably when he smiles.
- **Skin:** Clean and smooth-toned, olive-to-tan (#b8916a), healthy natural warmth rather than ruddiness — no broken capillaries or blotching. The eyebrow scar is the only break in an otherwise clear complexion.
- **Ears:** Clean, unmarked — no cauliflower ear, which tracks: the body came from the change, not from a career in the front row.

### Hair & Beard
- **Hair:** Dark brown/near-black (#2b2018), short and unstyled, greying only at the temples. Hairline slightly receded but not thinning on top. Thick, coarse, straight-to-faintly-wavy texture.
- **Beard:** Full salt-and-pepper (#6b6560) — a genuine grey-through-dark mix, rougher than groomed, roughly 3–4 weeks of maintained growth.

### Body
- **Body Hair:** Dense, all-over pelt — thick coverage across chest and stomach, continuing as a trail down the center of the abdomen, forearms, upper arms, back, and shoulders. No bare skin without looking for it.
- **Scarring:** None beyond the eyebrow scar, which predates the transformation — he took it as a young man during the bullying that drove him off the rugby team (see Origin). Otherwise unmarked.

## Gym / Method

Owen runs a gym. His method centers on a "protein shake" he gives his trainees — a drink that slowly draws out their bear form over a matter of weeks rather than all at once. Where the shake comes from is deliberately undecided for now.

His philosophy is the opposite of one narrow ideal. He doesn't reshape men toward a single accepted body type — the thing that was once done to him. He helps them become the best, biggest, most authentic version of themselves. Consent-forward and community-minded: post-workout barbecues, a circle of bears and cubs, an old-school coach who wants people to feel like they belong.

**Open / hooks:**
- Whether Owen can transform others directly, or whether the shake is the only vector, is not yet decided.
- A slow-acting drink handed to recruits and taking effect over weeks structurally echoes the Brotherhood's regional goo distribution — Owen's gym as the consensual mirror of it. The deferred "where does the shake come from" is a natural Chekhov's gun.

## Personality (Seed — Provisional, Not Yet Locked)

Working vibe only, to be developed properly later: warm, jovial, daddy-like. Old-school gym-coach energy — strong but not vain, confident and grounded, physically imposing without performing it. Protective toward cubs and newer gym-goers. Hosts post-workout barbecues, grills in the backyard, keeps a community of bears and cubs around him. Smiley by default, despite the gruff resting face.

## Overall Impression

A man who already carried size before the change and now carries it as power. Approachable face — barring the one old scar — on a body that takes up far more room than people expect. Reads as a genuine muscle bear rather than simply a big man: the mass is real, but so is the muscle under it, and he wears both like they finally belong to him.
