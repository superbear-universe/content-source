# Chapter 12 — Design Questionnaire

---

## Section 1: Opening — Mike's State of Mind

**1.1** Where is Mike when the chapter opens — physically, and in terms of his search?

He is at the kitchen table with Nick's notes again.

**1.2** How is Mike's grief expressing itself after five weeks?

A combination. He's frustrated and angry with himself for letting Nick get into danger once again. But also distraught, sad, and feeling hopeless for not being able to find him so far.

**1.3** Does Mike have anyone around him during this period, or has he been completely alone?

Mike has been almost entirely alone. He feels he can't share his feelings or any details about the situation — doing so risks exposing Nick's identity and his own. The isolation compounds everything.

**1.4** Is Mike still operating as Superbear during this time?

He hasn't been doing much superhero work. His mind is too preoccupied with finding Nick. Part of him feels guilty for not protecting the city, but he can't make himself care enough to act on it.

**1.5** What specific detail in the apartment or daily life anchors his loss?

Nick's glasses are still on the nightstand. Nick no longer needs them — the transformation took care of that — but they're still there, folded neatly, the way Nick left everything. Mike has been moving around them for five weeks without touching them.

---

## Section 2: The Investigation — How Mike Has Been Searching

**2.1** What has Mike already tried and failed?

- Super-smell (the trail is too old and goes underground — useless)
- Aerial surveillance
- Retracing Nick's steps
- Trying to reach Nick through their emotional/bear energy connection — nothing comes through, which terrifies him more than anything else
- Going through Nick's investigation notes again and again
- Going to the facility location Nick had identified in his notes — found the building completely deserted. The Brotherhood cleared out the entire operation without leaving a trace. They knew Nick's investigation had gotten that far.

**2.2** How does Mike feel about Nick's investigation notes?

He's been working through them obsessively. He went to the same location Nick had identified for Evan. The building was empty — completely cleared, not a single trace left. The Brotherhood moved when Nick got too close. This means Mike is further behind than he thought, and it also means Nick's notes were right. The investigation was ready. Mike just didn't listen.

**2.3** Is there a specific bottom — a last failed lead — before the thread to Daddy Bear appears?

The empty building is the wall. After that there is nothing left to try.

*Note: an earlier idea of contacting Nick's parents was considered and cut — it pulls the chapter sideways into an unestablished relationship dynamic and adds exposition that doesn't serve the story.*

**2.4** How does Mike find the thread that leads to Daddy Bear?

Hidden in the fold of the box holding Nick's notes — somewhere Mike hadn't looked — he finds the original note from chapter 1. The cheesy one: *"If you believe in yourself, you can be super."*

Nick had found it too at some point, and added annotations. He'd wondered who sent it and whether they had anything to do with what happened to Mike. He noted that the note had a distinctive smell — but his super-smell was still underdeveloped at the time, so he couldn't trace it and filed it as unresolved.

Mike brings the note to his nose. His super-smell is fully developed. The scent is there.

He feels stupid for forgetting about this — for never thinking about how everything started in the first place. He was busy being a superhero and falling for Nick. But finding Daddy Bear now feels like his last option.

*Note: Mike not thinking about the note sooner is not a character flaw. When he received it in chapter 1 it was just a cheesy motivational card — he had no reason to treat it as a supernatural lead. Nick only noticed the smell after developing super-smell, and even then couldn't trace it. Mike had no reason to go back to it until everything else had failed.*

---

## Section 3: Finding Daddy Bear

**3.1** Where does Mike find Daddy Bear?

A log cabin by a lake, outside the city. Daddy Bear lives like a hermit — isolated, self-sufficient, far from everything he used to be connected to.

**3.2** How long does it take Mike to find him once he has the thread?

Relatively quickly once he has the scent from the note.

**3.3** How does Mike travel there, and how does he approach?

Mike flies there in his suit — he can't risk being seen flying in the open air as himself. He lands near the cabin and de-transforms before walking the final stretch on foot. Daddy Bear sees a man in ordinary clothes come through the trees.

*Canon note confirmed: Mike and Nick have their powers at all times, suit or no suit. The suit is a separate layer. Mike wears it here purely because open sky travel risks exposing his identity.*

Daddy Bear is chopping logs when Mike arrives. He seems to expect him — there's a "well, that took you a while" quality to it.

**3.4** What makes Mike recognise this is the right person before anything is said?

His scent matches the note exactly. His build — heavyset once, now frail, but the frame is still there. And his overall energy: the quality of stillness, of someone who has been watching things for a very long time.

---

## Section 4: The Conversation

**4.1** Does Daddy Bear acknowledge Mike first, or does he let Mike speak? What does the very first exchange sound like?

Daddy Bear lets Mike speak. Mike references the note — something like acknowledging that he was trying to believe in himself but doesn't feel very super right now. This doubles as a signal to Daddy Bear that Mike knows about him, even if he doesn't fully understand who or what Daddy Bear actually is yet.

**4.2** What does the unlocking feel like for Mike — the first time in five weeks he's with someone who knows — and how does it come out?

Anger first, then frustration, then sadness. Mike finally vents everything he's been carrying alone. This is where he breaks — briefly, physically — and Daddy Bear pulls him into a bear hug. That hug is the warmth of the scene. Mike hasn't had anyone to hold onto for five weeks.

*Note: Mike only breaks once, here, at the beginning of the conversation when he first releases everything. He does not break again during the coffee scene. This was a problem in the previous draft.*

**4.3** Does Daddy Bear invite Mike inside, or does the conversation happen outside?

Daddy Bear offers to make Mike some coffee — regular this time — and they sit outside in front of the cabin with a view of the lake and the forest. Just nature around them. The distance from the city is part of what the setting gives the scene.

**4.4** Does Mike bring the note, and does it come up between them?

Mike references the text of the note as his opening signal (see 4.1). If the moment arises naturally they might talk about it further, but Mike has other things on his mind and the note doesn't need to become a centrepiece. It's a key, not a subject.

*On the note's actual purpose: Daddy Bear can admit he's not great at writing things like that. The cheesy text was cover. The real purpose of the note was always the scent — a built-in way for Mike to find him if he was ever needed. It was a failsafe, not a message.*

**4.5** What does Mike most need to hear in this conversation?

Two things: confirmation that the pulse he felt was real and that Nick is still in there — and that he can use that bear energy connection to find Nick and eventually reach him. Daddy Bear should make clear that Mike is stronger and more capable than he realises. He's been too consumed by grief to use what he has.

**4.6** Does Mike break — cry — during the conversation?

Only at the beginning, when he first vents (see 4.2). Not again after that. Once the hug happens and they sit down with coffee, Mike is present and functional, not suppressing ongoing collapse.

**4.7** How does Daddy Bear respond to Mike's grief physically?

Bear hug when Mike first breaks. Once they're sitting outside with coffee, a hand on Mike's knee at the right moment. Warm, grounded, paternal — present without managing.

**4.8** Why didn't Daddy Bear come to Mike sooner?

Daddy Bear's approach was always hands-off by design. He gave the coffee to someone he believed was worthy, then stayed close long enough to see whether Mike would use the power responsibly. Once he was satisfied that Superbear was doing his part, he retired to the cabin. His assumption was: if he's ever truly needed, Mike will find him through the note. The note was the failsafe.

He didn't come to Mike when Nick was taken because he assumed Mike would find his way to him sooner or later — and because he had genuinely stepped back from feeling like it was his place to intervene. Whether that was the right call is not something he defends strenuously.

*Note: the timeline between chapter 1 and now is deliberately unspecified. Mike reads as an experienced hero, but the exact duration doesn't need to be pinned down. It doesn't affect the chapter.*

**4.9** How does Mike piece together who Daddy Bear actually is?

He signals it with the opening line referencing the note. The recognition builds through the conversation as Daddy Bear's knowledge of how bear energy works becomes impossible to explain any other way. The note is the seed; the conversation is where it flowers. Daddy Bear doesn't make a dramatic announcement — Mike arrives at the understanding himself.

**4.10** What does Daddy Bear tell Mike about Nick?

Important constraint: Daddy Bear does not know Nick. He has no special knowledge of what happened to him or that he was corrupted. He gave away almost all of his power — he can't see or sense what the Brotherhood has done.

What he can do is reason from what he knows about bear energy and Bill. He can speculate that Bill likely corrupted Nick, explain what that would mean for his bear energy and why Mike can't feel him, and reason through what might still be reachable. This is informed speculation, not omniscience — and that distinction matters. They're figuring it out together, not receiving a briefing.

**4.11** Is there anything Daddy Bear won't tell Mike?

He's not ready to tell his full origin story yet. Not the right moment. That's for later.

**4.12** How does the conversation end?

Daddy Bear teaches Mike to channel and concentrate his bear energy — a short meditation-like sequence. At first Mike feels nothing. Then, faintly: a presence. A tiny warmth that is distinctively Nick. Daddy Bear teaches him to hold that feeling and use it like a compass. Mike now has a direction.

He thanks Daddy Bear and flies off. That is the end of the chapter.
