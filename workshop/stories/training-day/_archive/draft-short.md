Steam filled the bathroom in soft white clouds, curling against tile and glass until the small space felt suspended from the rest of the apartment. Water poured steadily from the shower head, warm and heavy, and Nick stood beneath it with his eyes closed, letting sensation arrive before thought.

Heat soaked into muscle, into thickness, into weight he was still learning to carry.

His hands moved slowly across his chest, spreading soap over skin that responded with a low, pleasant tingling. The broad plane of muscle beneath his palms felt solid and substantial, dark hair clinging in wet curls, and he traced the slope of his pecs down to where they met his shoulders with something close to reverence. Not for show—just because it felt impossibly good to exist inside this body.

The water caught in his beard next, heavier than he expected, and he laughed quietly as he shook his head and sent droplets scattering against the tile. A memory drifted up through the warmth: waking earlier with the mattress pressing firm into his thighs, his body feeling bigger even in sleep, the simple act of rolling over requiring more intention, more presence than it ever had before.

He smiled beneath the water, then reached carefully for the knob. Earlier that morning he'd nearly twisted it clean off the wall. Now he eased the pressure deliberately until the stream slowed, then stopped.

Steam rose as he stepped out. The mirror was fogged completely, his reflection nothing but a blurred, enlarged silhouette behind the glass.

He lifted one hand and drew a slow line through the condensation.

The image sharpened. Broad shoulders emerged first, then his chest, then his face—and Nick inhaled softly as the world suddenly came into focus with a clarity he hadn't experienced in years.

He blinked.

Perfectly clear.

"My glasses…" he murmured, glancing instinctively toward the nightstand in the other room where they still sat, abandoned and unnecessary.

The grout lines in the tile stood out in sharp definition. Droplets on the mirror caught light like tiny prisms. A quiet laugh escaped him as understanding settled in. Of course the transformation had fixed his eyesight. Everything else had changed—why not that?

He wrapped the towel low around his waist out of habit, the familiar gesture grounding the unfamiliar body, and studied his reflection properly for the first time.

Same eyes. Same shape to his smile. Different gravity behind both.

His beard framed his face thick and full, darker than before, water still clinging in the dense hair, and he ran his fingers through it with a quiet, pleased breath. The warmth and texture of it felt real in a way that made his chest tighten. His hand drifted lower, tracing the strong line of his jaw and down his throat where his pulse beat steady and deep.

Then he lifted one arm experimentally and flexed.

Muscle surged without effort, thickening and tightening beneath skin that stretched smoothly over power he was only beginning to understand. The movement felt natural, easy, like his body had finally learned a language it had been trying to speak for years.

"Whoa," he breathed.

He turned slightly, watching his shoulders roll with new weight, his torso shift with mass, the way the space around him adjusted to accommodate what he'd become. Water dripped steadily from his hair and beard, tapping soft rhythms against the tile, and a small, private smile curved his mouth.

This body wasn't pretending to be his.

It already was.

From the kitchen, faint sounds drifted in—a cupboard opening, a mug set down, the quiet hum of the coffee machine warming up. Life continuing, ordinary and patient, waiting for him to rejoin it.

Nick dried off slowly, then padded into the bedroom where Mike had laid out clothes on the bed.
